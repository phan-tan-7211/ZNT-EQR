**For:** Everyone  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-11

ZNTEQR does not publish a separate system status page. If the app looks broken or will not load, use the Support page instead.

## 1. Rule out a local problem

Reload the page and check that your device is online. If you use ZNTEQR on a phone in the field, confirm you have a signal before assuming the service is down.

## 2. Open the Support page

Go to [eqr.zinitek.com/support](https://eqr.zinitek.com/support) in any browser. You do not need to be signed in. The **Get Help** card lists the support email address and the expected response time.

## 3. Report the problem from the app when you can sign in

If you can still sign in, open the signed-in Support page at [eqr.zinitek.com/dashboard/support](https://eqr.zinitek.com/dashboard/support) and click **Report an Issue**. Session diagnostics attach automatically, and you can follow the ticket under **My Reported Issues**.
